About Us

What Is SSCS?

The Social Security Complaint Site (SSCS) is an independent platform designed to give patients and the public a safe, accessible channel to report concerns about social security health facilities.

Every submission is reviewed by trained administrators and forwarded to the appropriate oversight authority within 48 hours. We believe accountability starts with listening.
